# Website promotions

Owners and managers can open **Website settings → Manage promotions**. Choose an offer, audience and fulfilment type, then optionally set a minimum food subtotal, start and end times, an image, and whether to display the offer on the home and/or order page. Offers are disabled by default. Times are entered in the staff device's local time.

Item and category discounts apply to qualifying basket lines. A free-dish promotion discounts one qualifying dish already in the basket; it does not add a dish automatically. A bundle can require specific dishes, a specified number chosen from a list, or both. The fixed bundle price applies to one qualifying bundle per order. Free delivery discounts the delivery charge. Customers add the dishes using the regular menu.

The server selects the single best eligible promotion or entered promo code; they do not stack. The customer reviews the confirmed total before placing an order. Order snapshots retain the applied discount and promotion name.

Customer-specific promotions use a browser cookie to classify a visitor as returning after an order is confirmed. A missing cookie is treated as new. The same cookie value is sent to checkout for offer calculation, so it is a convenience measure, not proof of customer identity. Clearing cookies, using another device or changing the cookie can change eligibility. Promo codes configured as once per phone still use the phone number supplied with the order.
