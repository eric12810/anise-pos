Add a fee for each distance from the restaurant. The closest matching radius applies, measured in a straight line. Beyond the largest radius and up to 3 miles, the largest radius fee applies and staff must approve the order.

Optionally add sector charges, for example NW1 8. The higher of the matching radius fee and matching sector fee is charged. Sectors without a rule use the radius fee.
